# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want secretsift to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background,
identity or ability. Contributors and maintainers pledge to keep participation harassment-free and
respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior they judge to violate this code. To report a concern, use GitHub's private
reporting features on this repository or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in
good faith.
